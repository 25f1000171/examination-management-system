About KTA

Korrupt Testing Agency (KTA) has been established as a premier, specialist, autonomous and self-sustained testing organization to conduct entrance examinations for admission/fellowship in higher educational institutions.

To assess competence of candidates for admissions has always been a challenge in terms of matching with research based international standards, efficiency, transparency and error free delivery. The Korrupt Testing Agency is entrusted to address all such issues using best in every field, from test preparation, to test delivery and to test marking.
